Account and Data Deletion

Official procedure to request account deletion and data purge for CompraMás (developed by Arandu Labs LLC).

🛡️ Local-First Privacy Transparency

Your shopping lists, carts, and favorites reside locally on your device in SQLite. You can delete them by uninstalling the application or by requesting manual deletion below.

Steps to request deletion:

1

In-App Reset

Settings > Storage & Data > Clear local data to erase all records instantly.

2

Direct Email

Send an email to soporte@arandulabs.dev with subject "CompraMás Deletion".

3

Confirmation

Our engineering team processes and confirms complete deletion within 72 hours.
